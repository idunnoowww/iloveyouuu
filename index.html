For Ghiza

October 1st — A Space For Us

💌 1. Before Anything Else

i don't even know where to start, because somehow, every time i try to talk about you, words never feel like enough.

There is a quiet strangeness in how people enter our lives. Most people walk in and leave just as quickly, like footprints on a tide line that get washed away before the sun even sets. But you didn't do that.

You drifted into my world so gently that I didn't even notice the shift until one day, everything looked different because of you. What started as subtle, quiet exchanges slowly turned into a presence that felt warm, familiar, and completely irreplaceable.

✨ 2. How Did You Become This Important?

it's kind of funny when i think about it now. because i don't think either of us sat down one day and decided that we'd become this important to each other.

it just happened.

one conversation became another. one little thing became a memory. and somehow, somewhere between all those ordinary days, you became someone i started looking for in everything.

We never had a grand master plan. There was no single cinematic moment where music played in the background and lighting shifted. It was built in the silence between texts, the late-night thoughts shared when the rest of the world was asleep, and the random jokes that nobody else would understand.

I remember tiny details—things you probably mentioned in passing without thinking twice. The way your mood shifts, the things that make you quiet, the subtle soft side you protect so fiercely. Slowly, without permission, you became my default thought.

🤍 3. Things I Never Say Enough

Here are small truths I keep in my heart, pieces of how I see you every day:

01 — Your Existence

i don't think you realize how much your existence alone means to me. sometimes you don't even have to do anything. just knowing you're there is enough to make a bad day feel a little less heavy.

02 — The Little Things

i remember things you probably forgot five minutes after telling me. random words, tiny habits, the way you say certain things. i don't know why my brain keeps them, but it does. maybe that's what happens when someone matters too much.

03 — Your Softness

there's a softness in you that i don't think everyone gets to see. and i'm grateful that somehow, i've gotten to know that side of you.

04 — The Safe Space

In a world that constantly demands me to be loud, strong, and composed, being with you feels like finally taking off heavy armor. You make safe spaces out of thin air.

05 — Your Patience

Thank you for not walking away when I overthink or become hard to read. Your patient presence is a quiet comfort I never knew I needed.

06 — How You Listen

You don't just hear my words; you pay attention to what I don't say. That kind of understanding is rare, and I treasure it more than I can explain.

07 — Your Laughter

Whenever you laugh genuinely, it instantly softens whatever mood I'm in. It's easily one of my favorite sounds in the entire universe.

08 — The Distance We Bridge

Even when physical space or busy routines separate us, you never feel truly far away. You are grounded deeply in my mind.

09 — The Unspoken Comfort

We don't always need to fill every silence with words. Just existing in the same digital space or calm moment with you is enough.

10 — Your Genuine Heart

The way you care about people, even when you try to act tough, is beautiful. Your heart is far bigger than you let on.

11 — Choosing Each Other

Every single day, out of thousands of choices, deciding to stay connected with you is the easiest decision I ever make.

12 — Simply You

Without any pretense, without trying to impress anyone—just you, as you are. That version of you is my favorite person.

🥀 4. We Weren't Always Easy

i don't want to write about us like everything has always been perfect, because it hasn't.

we've misunderstood each other. we've gotten scared. we've pulled away. we've said things badly, stayed quiet when we should've talked, and sometimes made things harder than they needed to be.

but i think that's also why this means so much to me.

because despite all the messy parts, there was always something underneath it all that kept telling me that you mattered.

Real bonds aren't built on neat fairy tales where nothing goes wrong. They are forged in the uncomfortable moments—when we misunderstand each other, when distance gets frustrating, or when our own anxieties make us build walls.

The fact that we keep coming back, that we choose to figure things out instead of walking away, proves that what we have isn't fragile. It's real, grounded, and worth every difficult conversation.

🫀 5. The Part of Me That Gets Scared

i know i can be difficult sometimes.

i know i don't always say “i need you” when i actually do. sometimes i'd rather pretend i'm fine than admit that i want someone beside me. sometimes i pull away before anyone gets the chance to leave me first.

it's not because i don't care.

sometimes it's actually because i care too much.

and somehow, you're one of the few people who makes me want to put that stupid armor down, even if just for a little while.

Being vulnerable is terrifying. I've built habits around protecting myself, being hyper-independent, and convincing myself that relying on anyone is a risk.

But with you, Ghiza, those defenses feel tiring to hold up. You make me want to be honest about my fears, my chaotic thoughts, and my vulnerabilities. Thank you for giving me space to be imperfect without making me feel like I am too much to handle.

🌙 6. If I Could Keep One Thing…

if i could keep one thing from everything we've been through, i don't think i'd choose one specific day.

i'd choose the feeling.

the feeling of knowing there's someone somewhere who knows me a little more than most people do. someone whose name can make an ordinary day feel different. someone i can miss even while we're still talking.

that's probably the strangest part.

how can i miss someone who's still here?

It’s that quiet sensation of comfort that settles in my chest whenever I see your name pop up. It’s knowing that out of all the noise in the world, there is one person who feels like home.

💌 7. The Actual Love Letter

To my love, Ghiza,

I wanted to write this letter to you not just as a casual gesture, but as a genuine archive of everything I hold inside my head and often fail to say out loud.

How You Became Important

Looking back, I still don't fully understand the exact second everything shifted. But somewhere along the line, you ceased being just someone I talk to and became a core part of my daily life. Your presence settled in so deeply that now, when something funny, weird, or sad happens, my very first instinct is to tell you. You became my benchmark for comfort.

Things I Appreciate

I appreciate your spirit, your softness, and the unique perspective you bring into my world. I appreciate how you listen when I go on rants, and how you hold space for me even when I am distant. I love the way you react to small things, the humor we share, and the genuine warmth you radiate without even trying.

My Flaws & Fears

I know I am far from easy to handle. I have moments where I retreat into my shell, where my hyper-independence takes over, and where my fear of getting hurt makes me quiet. I get scared of feeling too much, scared of getting attached, scared of the unknown. But despite all of my defense mechanisms, you've remained a steady light. You never push me past my limits, yet you pull me out of my head just by being yourself.
